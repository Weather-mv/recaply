Welcome to the editor

1Add things from the left: auto captions, text, shapes, pictures, or the reaction layout for watch parties.
2Click to edit. Drag on the canvas to move, pull the handles to resize, the round handle to rotate. The panel on the right has every setting.
3Arrange in the timeline. Drag layers up or down to change what's in front, drag a bar to move it in time, its ends to trim. Right-click for more.
4Animate with In / Out effects, or ◆ keyframes at different times.
5Export when it looks right. Everything saves automatically.
