FAQ

Questions, answered

What counts as a clip?

One clip cut or exported from a project. Each is counted once: cutting the same clip again, re-exporting it or editing it further is free.

How does the free trial work?

Every new account gets 5 days and 5 clips with everything switched on. Shorts and exports carry a small Clipforge watermark until you upgrade.

What kinds of videos does it understand?

Podcasts, interviews, webinars, IRL and just-chatting streams, and gaming of every kind. Clipforge picks a content profile from the title and the first minutes of the video, and you can override it. Valorant broadcast round detection is still there as a profile.

What are story beats?

Labels for what each part of a clip does: hook, setup, tension, payoff, reaction or tangent. The AI reads the transcript to place them, and each gets its own score and reason. They show on the timeline and nudge the clip's ranking.

Do I need the cloud or an API key?

No. By default the AI runs locally through Ollama, which is free and private (install it and download a small model). If it isn't running, Clipforge still scores clips by loudness and transcript rules. Gemini, Claude or OpenAI keys are optional.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, then your account moves back to the free tier. Everything you made stays on your PC.

Where are my videos stored?

On your own PC. Clipforge is a Windows app: it downloads, analyses and renders everything locally, so there's no upload, no storage limit and nothing waiting in a queue. Only your account and plan are checked online.

What PC do I need?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) with 8 GB of RAM. An NVIDIA graphics card makes exports and the AI several times faster, but it isn't required.

What does faster processing on Pro mean?

Pro runs up to 6 clips at the same time (Starter 3, Free 2), so big batches finish much sooner.
