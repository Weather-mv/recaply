FAQ

Questions, answered

What counts as a clip?

One round downloaded from a recap, or one editor project exported. Each is counted once: downloading the same round again, re-exporting the same project or turning clips you already have into reels is free.

How does the free trial work?

Every new account gets 5 days and 5 clips with everything switched on, including AI captions. Reels and exports carry a small Recaply watermark until you upgrade.

Which VODs does Recaply understand?

Round detection is tuned for the VCT broadcast overlay and works on YouTube and Twitch VODs and live streams. For other videos, the loud-moments mode finds highlights from the audio.

What is a POV reel?

A vertical reel that fills the screen with the player's view, centred on the crosshair. Because that crops the corners away, Recaply cuts the kill feed out and places it back in the reel, so every kill stays readable.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, then your account moves back to the free tier. Everything you made stays on your PC.

Where are my videos stored?

On your own PC. Recaply is a Windows app: it downloads, analyses and renders everything locally, so there's no upload, no storage limit and nothing waiting in a queue. Only your account and plan are checked online.

What PC do I need?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) with 8 GB of RAM. Any NVIDIA graphics card makes reels and exports several times faster, but it isn't required.

What does faster processing on Pro mean?

Pro downloads up to 6 clips at the same time (Starter 3, Free 2), so big batches finish much sooner.
